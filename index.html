Who participates in performing arts events in Australia?

This page visualizes Australians' participation in performing arts events
using data from Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) and NSW Government.
These include live music, theatre, musicals, dance and others.

How many Australians go?

Live music is the most popular at about 18 in 100 Australian adults.
Whereas, dance performance is the least popular at about 5 in 100 Australian adults.

Source: ABS, Cultural and creative activities, 2021-22.

Which age group is amongst the audience?

Live music has concentrated amount of 18 to 24 year olds.
While, theatre is the steadiest across all age groups.
Furthermore, across all performing arts, people aged 75 and over
are the lest likely to go.

Source: ABS, Cultural and creative activities, 2021-22.

Do women go more than men?

For every type of performing arts, women do go more than men.
Women are found to almost be twice as likely to go to dance performances than men.
Whereas, live music is the most even between the two genders.

Source: ABS, Cultural and creative activities, 2021-22.

How participation has changed over time

Hollow circles: 2017-18 | Solid circles: 2021-22

For every type of performing arts, the attendance nearly halved
between the two years for both adults and children.

Sources: ABS, Attendance at Selected Cultural Venues and Events, 2017-18;
ABS, Cultural and creative activities, 2021-22.

Attendance by State

The Northern Territory has the highest percentage of 38.9%
Whereas, the lowest are NSW and Victoria at around 26%.

Source: ABS, Cultural and creative activities, 2021-22.

Attendance by City

New South Wales and Victoria has the biggest circles which
translates to having the most people that participated.
But, the states with biggest circles does not automatically mean
they often participate in performing arts. They just have more people.

Source: ABS, Cultural and creative activities, 2021-22.

The Before and After

Every state's attendance dropped. Across Australia,
the share dropped from 53.7% to 29.5%.

Source: ABS, Cultural and creative activities, 2021-22.

The drop based on NSW live performance attendance

The attendance in New South Wales dropped from 7.2 million in 2019
to 2 millions in 2020. The attendance recovered back to 9.2 million
in 2023. This is likely the result of the pandemic.

Source: Data.NSW, Number of attendees to live performances, NSW
